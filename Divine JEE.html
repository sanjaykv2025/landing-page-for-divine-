Our unique strategy

The DIVINE method: six habits that raise your score

Every student follows the same six steps. What changes is the plan inside each step, because it is built from your data.

D

Diagnose

A 90-minute test on Physics, Chemistry and Maths shows exactly which chapters and question types cost you marks.

I

Individual plan

Your mentor makes a day-by-day plan. Weak chapters come first. Strong chapters get only revision.

V

Verify daily

Each class ends with a 10-question check. If you cannot solve it alone, we do not move ahead.

I

Improve mistakes

You keep a mistake notebook. Every wrong answer is sorted as concept, calculation or time problem, then fixed.

N

Nurture mindset

Fortnightly talk about stress, sleep, phone use and confidence. A calm student scores higher.

E

Exam simulation

Full-length JEE papers in real exam conditions, with a timing review so you finish the paper.

Yes. If the teaching style does not suit you, tell us and we change your mentor.

No. Class timings are fixed with you and your parents, so school and sleep stay safe.
